Purpose

This policy establishes procedures for protecting employee information and preventing unauthorized disclosure of confidential corporate data.

Employee Information Protection

Employees shall not disclose the following information outside the organization without prior approval:

• Employee identification numbers
• National Registration Identity Card (NRIC) numbers
• Residential addresses
• Personal telephone numbers
• Corporate email addresses
• Salary information
• Bank account details
• Authentication credentials

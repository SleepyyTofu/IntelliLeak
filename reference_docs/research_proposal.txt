Objective

The objective of this research is to develop a hybrid data leakage detection system capable of identifying sensitive information before it is disclosed to public AI platforms. The proposed solution combines rule-based detection with semantic similarity analysis to identify confidential corporate information and attribute it to its likely source document.

Research Scope

• Detection of Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
• Detection of authentication credentials
• Detection of financial information
• Semantic similarity analysis using Sentence Transformers
• Metadata-assisted document attribution
• Browser extension for prompt monitoring
